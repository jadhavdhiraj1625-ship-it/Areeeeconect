  Surveyor Recruitment

How to Become an
AgriConnect Certified Surveyor

Follow the verified step-by-step process. Each phase must be completed and approved before proceeding. Only fully verified surveyors receive their login credentials.

Phase 1
Submit Online Application
Fill in your qualifications, experience, preferred taluka, civil license ID, and Aadhaar details via the secure application form.
Start Here — Open Now
Phase 2
Document Verification (HR)
Back-office HR reviews your submitted documents, license validity, educational certificates and field eligibility.
Unlocked after Phase 1
Phase 3
Technical Interview
A qualified operations officer conducts your technical interview covering field surveying, GPS boundary verification, and coordinate mapping.
Unlocked after Phase 2
Phase 4
Background Check
A criminal record and professional reference check is performed. Only candidates with a clean background proceed to onboarding.
Unlocked after Phase 3
Phase 5
Taluka Allocation
Based on district capacity, you are allocated to an available taluka. If capacity is full, you are placed on a Linked-List waitlist and onboarded when a vacancy arises.
Unlocked after Phase 4
Phase 6
Employee ID Issued
Upon successful allocation, an official AgriConnect employee ID (format AGR-YYYY-XXX) is assigned. You become an Active Certified Surveyor in the network.
Unlocked after Phase 5
Phase 7
Generate Login Password
Once verified as Active, visit the Applicant Portal and use the Password Generator with your registered mobile to create your secure surveyor credentials.
Only for Active Surveyors
Phase 8
Access Field Dashboard
Log in with your mobile and generated password to access your Surveyor Field Dashboard — accept survey jobs, upload GPS coordinates, and sign off field reports.
Unlocked after Phase 7
Apply as Surveyor Candidate
After applying, log in to the Surveyor Applicant Portal to track each step of your onboarding.
